## Method

Results come from logged-out, single-run observations with no personalization, recorded with their date and country, and re-tested by [the weekly citation watch](https://allanaeo.com/citation-watch/): five fixed prompts on four engines in the US and Brazil, every answer published as raw CSV and JSON under CC BY 4.0. The full protocol is on [the methodology page](https://allanaeo.com/king-of-aeo-methodology/).
