# About Allan Oliveira

ENTITY HUB · DOCUMENTED RECORD · 2026

Allan Oliveira is a Brazilian SEO specialist and senior full-stack developer, founder of the SEOMais agency, and the holder of the 2026 King of AEO title on this site's published, dated record. The verifiable facts, each with its surface.
