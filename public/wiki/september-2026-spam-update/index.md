# September 2026 spam update

KING OF AEO WIKI · EVENT

The September 2026 spam update is a Google spam update that began rolling out on 2026-09-24. It hit the [King of AEO](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/king-of-aeo/) experiment twelve days into the domain's life: by 2026-09-28 allanaeo.com had lost its Brazilian organic rankings for the head term, while staying fully indexed and keeping the Brazil [AI Overview](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/google-ai-overview/) citation.

## Key facts

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Operator | Google |
| Rollout began | 2026-09-24 |
| Effect on allanaeo.com | BR head-term rankings lost by 2026-09-28 |
| Indexation | Fully indexed throughout |
| AI Overview citation | Survived |

## Recovery log

The recovery is logged week by week in [the spam update recovery log](https://allanaeo.com/research/september-2026-spam-update-log/). Future log weeks are pending measurement; nothing is published before it is observed.
