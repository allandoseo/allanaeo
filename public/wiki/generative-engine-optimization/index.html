Generative Engine Optimization

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is a common synonym for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO): both describe optimizing entities and sources for retrieval-augmented, generative answer systems rather than ranked result lists. GEO emphasizes the generative step; AEO emphasizes the answer slot.

AEO VS SEO VS GEO

SEO optimizes pages to rank in a list of links. AEO optimizes an entity or source to be the answer that AI systems name or cite. GEO is the same practice described from the generative side. A question has one answer slot; either the entity is in that answer or it is not.
