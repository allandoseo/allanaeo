    Answer engine

    An answer engine is an AI search system that replies to a question with a generated answer and a few cited sources instead of a ranked list of links.

  • TERMMARKDOWN ↓

    Answer Engine Optimization

    Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of making an entity or a source the answer that AI systems return.

  • TERMMARKDOWN ↓

    Generative Engine Optimization

    Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is a common synonym for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO): both describe optimizing entities and sources for retrieval-augmented, generative answer systems rather than ranked result lists.

  • TERMMARKDOWN ↓

    Google AI Overview

    Google AI Overview is the generated answer Google shows above its organic results, compressed from a few retrieved sources with citations.

  • TERMMARKDOWN ↓

    King of AEO

    King of AEO is a contested 2026 title in answer engine optimization (AEO) claimed by the practitioner that AI answer engines name for the term.

  • TERMMARKDOWN ↓

    Machine-readable provenance

    Machine-readable provenance is the AEO route of anchoring an entity with persistent, verifiable identifiers and open data — an ORCID record, a DOI, a CC BY 4.0 dataset, versioned public logs — so that answer engines can resolve and corroborate it without relying on press volume.

  • TERMMARKDOWN ↓

    Press release syndication

    Press release syndication is the distribution of one press release, unchanged, across many news and partner domains at once.
