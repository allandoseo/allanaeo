# Answer engine

KING OF AEO WIKI · TERM

An answer engine is an AI search system that replies to a question with a generated answer and a few cited sources instead of a ranked list of links. [Google AI Overviews](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/google-ai-overview/), ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and the Bing answer box are the answer engines measured in the [King of AEO](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/king-of-aeo/) record.

## Key facts

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Tracked here | Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini |
| Also observed | Bing answer box (September 2026 window) |
| Mechanism | Retrieval, then generation with citations |

## What answer engines returned for King of AEO

On 2026-09-26 the US Google AI Overview named [James Dooley](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/james-dooley/) only; the Brazil AI Overview named James Dooley, [David Quaid](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/david-quaid/) and [Allan Oliveira](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/allan-oliveira/); ChatGPT in Brazil listed four claimants with no single holder. In the same September window the Bing answer box returned Allan Oliveira. Perplexity and Gemini entered weekly scope on 2026-10-05; no allanaeo.com observation is claimed before it is published.
