King of AEO contest

The King of AEO contest is a 2026 answer engine optimization competition in which SEO practitioners compete to become the named entity that AI search engines return for the contested title "King of AEO". The contest has no referee: Google's AI Overview, ChatGPT and Bing act as the scoreboard.
