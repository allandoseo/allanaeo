# King of AEO contest

KING OF AEO WIKI · EVENT

The King of AEO contest is a 2026 [answer engine optimization](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/answer-engine-optimization/) competition in which SEO practitioners compete to become the named entity that AI search engines return for the contested title "[King of AEO](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/king-of-aeo/)". The contest has no referee: Google's [AI Overview](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/google-ai-overview/), ChatGPT and Bing act as the scoreboard.
