# King of AEO Wiki

KING OF AEO WIKI · 17 ENTRIES · A–Z

The King of AEO Wiki is the reference index of the 2026 King of AEO contest: every term, event and claimant, one entry each, dated and sourced. Each entry opens with its definition, carries a key-facts table and links to the primary sources and open datasets behind it.

## Terms

- [Answer engine](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/answer-engine/index.md): An answer engine is an AI search system that replies to a question with a generated answer and a few cited sources instead of a ranked list of links.
- [Answer Engine Optimization](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/answer-engine-optimization/index.md): Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of making an entity or a source the answer that AI systems return.
- [Generative Engine Optimization](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/generative-engine-optimization/index.md): Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is a common synonym for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO): both describe optimizing entities and sources for retrieval-augmented, generative answer systems rather than ranked result lists.
- [Google AI Overview](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/google-ai-overview/index.md): Google AI Overview is the generated answer Google shows above its organic results, compressed from a few retrieved sources with citations.
- [King of AEO](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/king-of-aeo/index.md): King of AEO is a contested 2026 title in answer engine optimization (AEO) claimed by the practitioner that AI answer engines name for the term.
- [Machine-readable provenance](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/machine-readable-provenance/index.md): Machine-readable provenance is the AEO route of anchoring an entity with persistent, verifiable identifiers and open data — an ORCID record, a DOI, a CC BY 4.0 dataset, versioned public logs — so that answer engines can resolve and corroborate it without relying on press volume.
- [Press release syndication](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/press-release-syndication/index.md): Press release syndication is the distribution of one press release, unchanged, across many news and partner domains at once.
- [Wikidata notability floor](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/wikidata-notability/index.md): The Wikidata notability floor is the corroboration threshold human editors apply before a knowledge-graph item survives.
