# Press release syndication

KING OF AEO WIKI · TERM

Press release syndication is the distribution of one press release, unchanged, across many news and partner domains at once. In the [King of AEO contest](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/king-of-aeo-contest/) a release was syndicated to 312 domains for $6 on 2026-09-24 — release IDs 36819–37132, identical text and date on every domain: one source wearing 312 hats.

| Outcome | Volatility; head-term rankings lost by 2026-09-28 |

## Why syndication stopped working

Google's [September 2026 spam update](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/september-2026-spam-update/) began rolling out the same day, 2026-09-24. By 2026-09-28 the domain had lost its Brazilian head-term rankings while staying fully indexed. Duplicate syndication bought volatility, not durable authority. [James Dooley](https://allanaeo.com/wiki/james-dooley/)'s route differs in kind: years of accumulated English-language coverage, not a single release. The mechanics are reverse engineered in [the $6 press release study](https://allanaeo.com/research/6-dollar-press-release/).
