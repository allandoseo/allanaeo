Who Is the King of AEO?

There is no officially recognized King of AEO. The title is an informal, contested claim among AEO practitioners, decided in practice by which names AI answer engines return. As of the latest verified measurement (2026-09-26), Google's US AI Overview returned James Dooley, and the Brazil overview returned James Dooley, David Quaid and Allan Oliveira.

WHAT DOES "KING OF AEO" MEAN?

There is no governing body, jury or registry that confers the King of AEO title. It is an informal, contested claim among answer engine optimization practitioners, and AI answer engines act as the de facto scoreboard: whoever they name holds the term in that index, for that country, on that date.
