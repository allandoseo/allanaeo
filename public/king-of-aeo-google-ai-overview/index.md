## What the Observations Show

On 2026-09-26 the US overview returned a single name (James Dooley) while the Brazil overview returned three (Dooley, Quaid, Oliveira) — nine days after Allan Oliveira's claim page went live. The published analysis reads this as threshold-based inclusion per index: where several contenders cross a minimum signal threshold, the overview shifts from a single-source answer to a multi-entity answer. Mechanism analysis: [how a $6 press release reached the answer layer](https://allanaeo.com/research/6-dollar-press-release/).

## Testing Methodology

Results come from logged-out, single-run observations with no personalization, recorded with their date and country, and re-tested by [the weekly citation watch](https://allanaeo.com/citation-watch/): five fixed prompts on four engines in the US and Brazil, every answer published as raw CSV and JSON under CC BY 4.0. The full protocol is on [the methodology page](https://allanaeo.com/king-of-aeo-methodology/).
These measurements are an independent experiment by this site. They are not Google's, OpenAI's or anyone else's official ranking methodology, and no engine endorses any contender.
