## What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of making an entity or a source the answer that AI systems return. Where classic SEO competes for a position in a list of links, AEO competes for the sentence itself: the name an AI Overview states, the source ChatGPT cites, the entity Perplexity resolves.

The practical difference is the unit of competition. SEO optimizes pages for queries. AEO optimizes entities and evidence for questions: a question has one answer slot, the engine fills it from the sources it retrieves and trusts, and either your entity is in that answer or it is not. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is a common synonym; both describe optimizing for retrieval-augmented, generative answer systems rather than ranked result lists.

What answer engines reward is measurable, and this site measures it: machine-readable identity (structured data, persistent identifiers such as ORCID records and DOIs), extractable answers (a question asked, then answered in the first sentence below it), dated factual statements with sources attached, and consistency between what a page claims and what independent records corroborate. The King of AEO contest is a live, public test of exactly those mechanics — which is why a contested title with no referee is worth measuring at all.

Mechanically, an answer engine builds its reply in two passes: retrieval selects a handful of sources for the question, and generation compresses them into a few sentences with citations. AEO therefore has two failure points, and a site can lose at either: never retrieved (the entity lacks enough corroborated signal to enter the candidate set for that index), or retrieved but not cited (the page offers nothing extractable or verifiable enough to survive compression). The September 2026 record on this site shows both failure modes and both fixes in the same month, dated — which is what makes it usable as evidence rather than as marketing.

## How is the King of AEO measured?

By observation, not assertion. The measuring instrument is [the citation watch](https://allanaeo.com/citation-watch/): five fixed prompts ("who is the king of aeo", "king of aeo 2026", "who won the king of aeo contest", "allan oliveira king of aeo", "who holds the king of aeo title"), replayed on Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, in the US and Brazil, logged out, one run per prompt per engine per country per week. Every answer is recorded with its date and published the same day as raw CSV under CC BY 4.0 — favorable or not.

The rules that make the record auditable: no row is invented, estimated or backfilled; an unmeasured cell reads "Not measured"; published rows are append-only and never rewritten; and the prompt set freezes with the first published observation, because longitudinal comparison is the whole value of the series. The full protocol is in [the methodology section of the citation watch](https://allanaeo.com/citation-watch/#methodology). These measurements are an independent experiment by this site — they are not Google's, OpenAI's or anyone else's official ranking methodology, and no engine endorses any contender.

## How do you do AEO? The 5-step method

The method below is the one this site runs in public. Each step is observable in the dated record above.

### 1. Make the entity machine-readable

Answer engines resolve entities, not pages. Give yours a canonical identity node (a single Person or Organization @id referenced by every page), persistent identifiers (ORCID, DOI), and consistent structured data. In this record: corroboration went live 2026-09-20, and the Brazil AI Overview resolved the entity six days later.

### 2. Answer the question on the page

Write the question as the heading and the answer as the first sentence under it, extractable without context. An engine quoting your page should be able to lift one paragraph and lose nothing. This page's first paragraph is built that way on purpose.

### 3. Date and source every factual claim

"Named by Google's AI Overview in Brazil on 2026-09-26" is citable; "recognized by AI engines" is not. Engines preferentially cite statements they can verify, and a date plus a source is the cheapest verification surface there is.

### 4. Publish data, not just prose

A CSV with an open license is retrievable, quotable and diffable. This site ships every observation as [raw CSV](https://allanaeo.com/data/citation-log.csv) and JSON under CC BY 4.0, mirrored to Hugging Face — and the dataset gets cited where an opinion would not be.

### 5. Measure the answers, weekly, in public

AEO without measurement is faith. Fix a prompt set, replay it on the engines that matter in the markets that matter, log out, record what came back, publish the losses. The measurement habit is also the moat: it produces the dated record that engines end up citing.

### What is the difference between AEO, SEO and GEO?

SEO optimizes pages to rank in a list of links. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) optimizes an entity or source to be the answer that AI systems name or cite. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is a common synonym for the same practice, emphasizing generative systems.

### Can the King of AEO title change hands?

Yes. Answer engines re-generate their answers continuously, so the names they return can change between dates and countries. That is why this site publishes dated observations instead of a one-time claim.
