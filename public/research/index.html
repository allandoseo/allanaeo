About this archive

This archive exists because the 2026 King of AEO contest produced something rare: a contested title whose every move left a dated, measurable trace. Seven claimants, three signal strategies, two search indices, one spam update — and a public record of what each tactic actually did to the answers engines return. Each study below is built from the same open data the rest of the site publishes: the September experiment window (17–28 September 2026), the weekly citation watch, and the hand-verified claim record. Studies are published with their raw CSV attached, corrected in place with a dated changelog, and mirrored as Markdown for machine retrieval.
