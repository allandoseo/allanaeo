2026 COMPETITION OVERVIEW

There is no governing body, jury or registry that confers the King of AEO title. It is an informal, contested claim among answer engine optimization practitioners, and AI answer engines act as the de facto scoreboard: whoever they name holds the term in that index, for that country, on that date.

2026 is the year the claims became measurable: dated public claims (the earliest documented is 2026-08-31), a September measurement window with published observations, and from October a weekly fixed-prompt series.

The field is wider than three: on the same date, ChatGPT in Brazil listed four contenders with no single holder. This site adds a contender to the record only when a dated public claim has been verified at its source.

METHODOLOGY

Results come from logged-out, single-run observations with no personalization, recorded with their date and country, and re-tested by the weekly citation watch: five fixed prompts on four engines in the US and Brazil, every answer published as raw CSV and JSON under CC BY 4.0. The full protocol is on the methodology page.

These measurements are an independent experiment by this site. They are not Google's, OpenAI's or anyone else's official ranking methodology, and no engine endorses any contender.
