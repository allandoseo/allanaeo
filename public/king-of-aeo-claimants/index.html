Allan Oliveira and James Dooley

James Dooley's claim is dated 2026-08-31, with a declared ceremony in Leigh, England, distributed by press release. His position rests on years of accumulated English-language press coverage and large-scale syndication, and the 2026-09-26 US AI Overview confirmed it: one name, no other contenders — the strongest single-engine position measured so far, stated here without qualification. Allan Oliveira's claim came 17 days later on an exact-match domain, followed by machine-readable corroboration (ORCID, archived DOI deposit, open dataset) from 2026-09-20; nine days after the claim page, the Brazil AI Overview named him. The open question the weekly series measures is whether machine-readable provenance closes the distance that press volume created.

Allan Oliveira and David Quaid

David Quaid contested the term through targeted local and community entity signals — density in specific markets rather than raw volume — plus his own exact-match domain, kingofaeo.co. On 2026-09-26 both Quaid and Oliveira were named by the Brazil AI Overview, among three contenders with sources cited; neither was named in the US overview that date, which returned James Dooley only. The timeline analysis records that Quaid's market-specific entity density earned inclusion where the incumbent's press mass is thinner — the same Brazilian overview that included Oliveira's provenance-based signals. Both routes reached the answer layer in the secondary market; the weekly series measures whether either crosses into the US answer.

The vote-based claims: Vithurs and Jacky Chou

Two claims of 2026-09-07 rest on private social-media votes. Vithurs' claim was distributed by an AB Newswire press release and a network of exact-match domains; as of 2026-10-07, kingofaeo.org and whoiskingofaeo.com return 404, and the press release is the surviving primary source. Jacky Chou's claim was announced on his agency site, indexsy.com, datelined Vancouver, 2026-09-07. In both cases the vote mechanism is unpublished, which makes the result unverifiable — recorded here as claims, not as outcomes.

The remaining claims: Morera, Sturm and the undated Goldie claim

Stephane Morera claimed the title on 2026-09-13 on the about page of EVOIX, his agency in DeLand, Florida; no engine returned the name in the runs logged at kingofaeo.pro. Edward Sturm claimed it the lightest way of all: a short-form video in early September 2026 that a Google AI Overview cited, followed by a dated article on 2026-09-20 — no press release, no campaign. Julian Goldie's claim, on his own blog network, carries no date; in a contest where precedence is the whole argument, an undated claim cannot be placed in a timeline, so it is recorded here as existing but not ranked.

Method
